About Shine Mobile Valet

Background

Shine Mobile Valet started in 2021. The owner, Thabo, used to work at a car dealership in Midrand. He spent years watching people bring in dirty cars and pick them up looking brand new. He realised there was demand for a mobile service that comes to your home or office instead of you having to go to a car wash.

He started small with just a van and basic equipment. Now he has one employee helping him and a solid base of regular customers. The business operates around Johannesburg and Pretoria.

Our Mission

To provide professional, convenient car detailing that comes to your door at a fair price.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted mobile valet service in Gauteng.

Who We Serve

  • Working professionals in Sandton, Centurion, and surrounding areas who don't have time to visit a car wash
  • Families with kids who need regular interior cleaning
  • People selling their cars who want them looking showroom-ready
  • Small businesses with 2-3 vehicles who want regular cleaning
